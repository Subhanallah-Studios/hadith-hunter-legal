Subhanallah Studios ("Subhanallah Studios," "we," "us," or "our") operates the mobile game Hadith Hunter (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the App, how it is used, and the choices available to you.
Questions? Contact us at support@subhanallahstudios.com 
Information We Collect
Hadith Hunter does not require account creation, and we do not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, or other directly identifying information to play.
The App and the third-party services integrated into it may process the following:
a) Device, Advertising, and Ad-Interaction Data
The App uses Google AdMob to serve advertising, including interstitial ads and optional rewarded ads. Depending on your age, region, device settings, consent choices, and applicable law, Google AdMob may process:
Device and operating-system information
IP address
General location derived from IP address
Advertising identifiers, where permitted
Ad impressions, clicks, interactions, and related advertising data
For users who receive child or age-unknown treatment, advertising identifiers and personalized advertising are restricted or disabled in accordance with platform requirements and Google advertising policies.
b) Purchase Information
Optional in-app purchases (coin packs and "Remove Ads") are processed through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store, with purchasing facilitated by Unity In-App Purchasing.
We do not receive or store your payment-card or banking information. We may receive limited transaction or confirmation data needed to:
Confirm a purchase completed
Deliver purchased in-game content
Restore eligible purchases
Help prevent fraud
c) Locally Stored Game Data
Certain game information is stored locally on your device, including Hadith Journal progress, badges and achievements, coin balance, unlocked skins or items, settings and preferences, selected language, and other game progress.
This data remains on your device and is not transmitted to us.
We do not run our own gameplay analytics, and we do not collect a name, email, contacts, photos, or precise location.
Aggregate crash or performance information may be made available to us by the Apple App Store or Google Play at the operating-system level only if you have enabled sharing such data with developers through your device settings. We do not collect this information through an in-app analytics SDK.

Children's Privacy
We take children's privacy seriously and seek to comply with applicable children's-privacy laws, including COPPA where applicable, and platform requirements.
We do not knowingly ask children to provide their name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photographs, or other directly identifying information.
Where the App is used by children or users of unknown age, advertising and data processing are restricted in accordance with applicable law and platform requirements. Child-directed or age-restricted treatment disables personalized advertising and restricts the use or transmission of advertising identifiers.
Google requires apps targeting both children and adults to prevent advertising identifiers from being transmitted for children and unknown-age users, and mixed-audience apps may present a neutral age screen.
If you believe we have collected personal information from a child in a manner not permitted by law, contact support@subhanallahstudios.com, and we will review the matter and take appropriate action.
